# Space Distortion Boss

**A cosmic horror boss for Terraria (Bunny Loader)**

The **Space Distortion** is a post-Moon Lord boss that warps reality itself. Summon it using the **Cosmic Rift Key** at night to face a multi-phase battle against a entity that tears through dimensions.

## Summoning

Craft the **Cosmic Rift Key** at the **Ancient Manipulator** using:
- 18 × Vortex Fragments
- 10 × Luminite Bars
- 5 × Soul of Might
- 5 × Soul of Sight
- 5 × Soul of Fright

Use the key **at night** to summon the boss. It cannot be summoned during the day.

## Boss Behavior

### Phase 1 (100% - 50% HP)
- **Void Charge**: Dashes at the player with a telegraph
- **Void Bolt Barrage**: Orbits the player while firing homing void bolts
- **Distortion Wave**: Releases expanding rings that pull players inward

### Phase 2 (50% - 10% HP)
*Triggered at 50% HP with a dramatic transformation*
- **Black Hole Summoning**: Creates gravitational singularities that pull and damage
- **Star Rain**: Rains cosmic shards from above
- **Void Charge (Enhanced)**: Leaves black holes in its wake

### Enraged Phase (Below 10% HP)
*All attacks combined with increased speed and damage*
- 50% increased damage
- 20% reduced defense
- Constant barrage of all attack types

## Recommended Preparation

- **Armor**: Solar Flare, Vortex, or Nebula armor (post-Moon Lord)
- **Weapons**: High DPS ranged/magic weapons (Phantasm, Last Prism, SDMG)
- **Accessories**: Celestial Shell, Ankh Shield, Wings, Master Ninja Gear
- **Buffs**: Ironskin, Regeneration, Endurance, Lifeforce, Wrath/Rage
- **Arena**: Large flat platform with heart/star statues, campfire, heart lantern
